# Voice Checklist

Voice Checklist supports saved sequences of instructions and guided playthroughs of those sequences.

## Language

**Routine**:
A saved, ordered list of steps.
_Avoid_: Checklist, list

**Step**:
One instruction within a routine.
_Avoid_: Item, task, todo

**Run**:
One playthrough of a routine, distinct from the saved routine itself.
_Avoid_: Session, playback
